Kimluna Publisher Privacy Policy

Kimluna Publisher is a tool operated by Nuts studio to connect and manage authorized Instagram and Threads accounts for studio publishing. This policy covers that connection, not Meta's own processing or the studio's unrelated services.

What the app uses

When an account is connected, the app uses information returned by Meta, including account identifiers, usernames, and access tokens. Token records can include token expiry and connection timestamps. For publishing, the app can send an operator-selected caption and media to the selected Meta service and retain publication identifiers or links to verify the result. The app does not ask for or store Meta account passwords.

How the app uses and stores this information

The app uses this data to authorize the connection, verify that the intended account is connected, publish content selected by the operator, and check publishing results. Access tokens are stored encrypted on the operator's Windows system. The app sends the data needed for those operations to Instagram, Threads, or Meta as applicable. Connected-account data is not sold or used by this app for advertising. Connection records are retained while needed to operate or verify the connection; deletion can be requested below.

Disconnecting and requesting deletion

To stop access, remove Kimluna Publisher in your Meta, Instagram, or Threads connected-app settings where the app is listed. To request deletion of data this app stores, email kahvilavalas@gmail.com with the subject “Kimluna Publisher data deletion” and include the platform and username. Do not send a password or access token. The operator may verify account ownership before removing the stored connection record and any associated token copies. Disconnecting the app or deleting its data does not automatically delete posts already on Instagram or Threads; manage those posts directly on the service.

Contact

For questions about this app's handling of account data, email kahvilavalas@gmail.com.
